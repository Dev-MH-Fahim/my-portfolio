Better beginnings.

A few practical answers about planning and building a website. Confirm availability, timelines, and commercial terms directly before making commitments.

What kinds of projects do you take on?

WordPress websites, WooCommerce storefronts, redesigns, and focused website improvements. The right scope depends on your goals and the work required.

Can I update the website myself?

The intention is to make common content updates manageable through WordPress and its block editor. The editing experience should be designed around the content your team actually maintains.

How long does a website take?

Timing depends on scope, content readiness, feedback, and integrations. Agree on a realistic timeline after discovery and before work begins.

How much does a project cost?

Pricing depends on requirements and deliverables. Share the goals, essential features, and any budget range in your inquiry so the scope can be discussed clearly.

Can you work with an existing website?

Yes, a project may involve improving an existing WordPress site, addressing specific issues, or planning a larger redesign. The current setup needs to be reviewed first.

Does the contact form send my message?

This static portfolio prepares a message in your email application. It does not send or store form data itself. A production server-side form requires secure delivery and spam protection.
