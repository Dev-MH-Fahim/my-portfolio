THE CHALLENGE

Help visitors find the important things.

Local businesses often need one page to serve many kinds of visitors: people checking a service, looking for location information, or deciding whether to make contact. The concept focuses on structuring those essentials so they are easy to scan.

THE APPROACH

Put the next step within reach.

A direct introduction, concise service overview, and clear contact path provide a foundation for a useful small-business website. For a real redesign, customer questions and existing analytics should guide the final content order.

  • Plain-language introduction and service summaries.
  • Consistent navigation and contact details.
  • Readable typography and small-screen layout.
