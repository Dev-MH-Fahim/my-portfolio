Cookies & local storage

Know what lives
in your browser.

Last reviewed: September 2026 · Starter content for review.

Review this page against the deployed version, hosting configuration, and local requirements. A consent banner or further disclosure may be needed if analytics, embeds, or marketing technologies are added.

Display preference

This starter website stores your selected light or dark theme in local storage when browser storage is available. The preference remains on your device and can be cleared through browser settings.

Cookies and external resources

The portfolio code does not intentionally set a cookie. Google Fonts is loaded as an external resource, and the eventual web host or any later-added tools may use their own technologies. Confirm their behavior and document it accurately after deployment.

Manage browser data

You can clear local storage and cookies through your browser’s privacy or site settings. Clearing the theme preference returns the appearance to your system setting.
