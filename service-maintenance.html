A careful first look at a WordPress or frontend issue, followed by a clear plan for the fix or next improvement.

FIX THE CAUSE, NOT JUST THE SYMPTOM

Start with a clear diagnosis.

Describe what changed, what you expected, and what you have already tried. A review can help establish whether the issue is a content problem, a theme or plugin conflict, an integration, or something else.

Access, backups, urgency, and the current site setup should be agreed before work begins. Security-sensitive credentials should never be sent in an ordinary contact message.
