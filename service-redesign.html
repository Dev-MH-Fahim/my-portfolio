REVIEW BEFORE REBUILD

Improve the right things, not everything.

A useful redesign begins by looking at the current site, its content, and how visitors use it. Keep valuable material and working features; prioritize the real sources of confusion and friction.

Where available, analytics and user feedback can help establish a baseline. Performance or conversion improvements should be measured rather than promised in advance.
